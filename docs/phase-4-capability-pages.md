## Content discipline
- Customer logos/names: only from approved corporate source.
- Customer-specific engineering claims: only when supported by approved public or internal evidence.
- Use representative engineering experience when customer identity cannot be publicly disclosed.
- Do not imply that every listed customer uses every listed AionSi capability.
- Avoid unsupported metrics, outcomes, or technology-node claims.

## Capability-page quality gate
Every capability page should include:
- clear engineering problem and customer outcome
- concise technical scope
- methodology/engineering workflow
- evidence section with working links where approved evidence exists
- engagement-model explanation
- standardized CTA: `Discuss Your Engineering Requirement →`
- no broken links or placeholder evidence
- desktop 100% review and mobile responsive review
